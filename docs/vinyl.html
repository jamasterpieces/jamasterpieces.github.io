Materials We Cut

  • Adhesive Vinyl: decals, stickers, labels, window graphics.
  • Heat Transfer Vinyl (HTV): shirts, tote bags, simple apparel graphics.
  • Cardstock & Paper: cards, inserts, tags, packaging accents.
  • Stencil Film / Masks: paint stencils for signs, walls, and projects.

Best Uses

  • Laptop decals, water bottles, gear labels, and custom stickers.
  • Shop hours, door logos, and small signs for local businesses.
  • Event shirts, team or family shirts, and simple HTV apparel.
  • Paper cuts for cards, tags, layered décor, and packaging.

Care & Durability

  • Hand-wash items with decals; avoid dishwashers unless specified.
  • Allow 24 hours after install before heavy cleaning or handling.
  • Outdoor use works best on smooth, clean, non-flaking surfaces.

Surface & Application Disclaimer

Vinyl decals, stickers, and applied graphics rely on the condition of the surface they are applied to. J & A Masterpieces is not responsible for damage to paint, finishes, walls, vehicles, or other surfaces caused by improper installation, unstable paint/clear coat, incompatible materials, or the removal of decals.

For best results, apply only to clean, smooth, fully cured surfaces. Avoid applying to peeling paint, textured walls, or surfaces exposed to extreme heat or prolonged moisture.

Artwork & File Types

Vector files (SVG, AI, PDF) are ideal for the cleanest cuts. High-resolution PNG/JPG (at least 300 DPI) also works for printed stickers and basic shapes. Simple text-based designs can be set in-house.

How It Works

  1. Share your idea — what you need, how many, sizes, and where it will go.
  2. Artwork check — send your files or describe your idea; we’ll confirm what’s needed.
  3. Proof — we send a digital mockup for approval before cutting.
  4. Cut & finish — we print (if needed), cut, weed, and prep for pickup or shipping.
  5. Apply or install — DIY with simple instructions, or ask about local installation.
