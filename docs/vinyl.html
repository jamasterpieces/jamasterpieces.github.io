Vinyl Types

  • Permanent/Outdoor: durable adhesive for mugs, cars, signs.
  • Removable/Indoor: lower‑tack for walls and temporary displays.
  • Specialty: matte, gloss, metallic, holographic, translucent.

Best Uses

  • Car windows, laptop decals, water bottles, gear labels
  • Shop hours and door logos
  • Wall quotes and seasonal decor

Care

  • Hand‑wash items with decals; avoid dishwashers.
  • Allow 24 hours after install before heavy cleaning.
  • Outdoor decals last longer on smooth, clean surfaces.

Surface & Application Disclaimer

Vinyl decals and stickers rely on the condition of the surface they are applied to. J & A Masterpieces is not responsible for damage to paint, finishes, walls, vehicles, or other surfaces caused by improper installation, unstable paint/clear coat, incompatible materials, or the removal of decals.

For best results, apply only to clean, smooth, fully cured surfaces. Avoid applying to peeling paint, textured walls, or surfaces exposed to extreme heat or prolonged moisture.

Artwork & File Types

Vector files (SVG, AI, PDF) are ideal. High‑resolution PNG/JPG (at least 300 DPI) also works. Simple text can be set in‑house.
