CEEC BRACELET FAMILIES

What's the difference?

Acrylic

Colorful • Lightweight • Everyday

Acrylic bracelets are built around bright, playful beads and are usually made on stretch cord for easy everyday wear.

Best for
Color, names, themes, and casual wear
Closure
Usually stretch
Custom options
Colors, wording, accent beads, and size

Polymer Clay

Handmade • Artistic • One-of-a-kind

Polymer clay bracelets feature handmade character and unique color combinations. Small differences are part of the design.

Best for
Unique color combinations and artistic pieces
Closure
Usually stretch
Custom options
Color direction and size, depending on available beads

Nylon Paracord

Durable • Utility-inspired • Customizable

CEEC's traditional paracord bracelets use nylon cord and are built for durability and everyday toughness.

Best for
Durability, outdoor-inspired style, and structured designs
Closure
Clip or specialty hardware, depending on design
Custom options
Colors, knot style, clip type, and size

Nylon paracord is the material used for CEEC's custom survival-style paracord bracelets.

Soft Paracord

Comfort-first • Soft • Limited-run

Soft paracord uses polyester cord for a gentler feel and playful color combinations. These are treated as special-edition pieces.

Best for
Comfort, softness, and casual everyday wear
Closure
Varies by design
Availability
Ready-made and limited runs

Soft paracord bracelets are not marketed as survival, tactical, or load-bearing gear.

Fantasy

Statement • Detailed • Character-inspired

Fantasy bracelets are special designs that lean into decorative hardware, dramatic clasps, themed details, or unusual construction.

Best for
Statement pieces and distinctive designs
Closure
Specialty hardware or clasps
Availability
Limited and design-dependent
