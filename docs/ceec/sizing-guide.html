GET THE RIGHT FIT

Sizing Guide ♡

A good bracelet fit starts with a good wrist measurement. Use this guide before ordering a ready-to-ship or custom CEEC bracelet.

WHAT YOU NEED

Just a flexible measuring tape.

No measuring tape? Wrap a piece of string around your wrist, mark the overlap, then measure the string with a ruler.

HOW TO MEASURE

Measure your wrist in three quick moves.

1

Wrap

Wrap the measuring tape around the wrist where you normally wear a bracelet.

2

Keep it snug

The tape should sit against your skin without squeezing or leaving extra slack.

3

Record it

Write down the wrist measurement in inches and compare it with CEEC's sizing chart below.

FIT PREFERENCE

Snug, comfortable, or loose?

Snug

Fits close to the wrist with minimal movement. Best for people who prefer bracelets to stay in place.

Comfortable

A little movement without feeling loose. This is the safest default if you are unsure.

Loose

More movement around the wrist. Mention this preference on a custom order so Carmen can account for it.

BETWEEN SIZES?

Tell us your wrist measurement.

If you fall between two CEEC sizes, include your actual wrist measurement and preferred fit on the custom order form rather than guessing.
