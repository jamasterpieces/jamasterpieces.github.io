ORDER SUPPORT

Shipping & Returns ♡

Here is what to expect after placing an order with CEEC, including processing, shipping, delivery, and return support.

On This Page

Processing Shipping Delivery Issues Returns Custom Orders Damaged Items Need Help?

BEFORE IT SHIPS

Processing Times

Ready-to-Ship Items

Ready-to-ship items are prepared and shipped within 48 hours of receiving the order.

Made-to-Order Items

Made-to-order and custom items require additional production time. If special materials must be obtained to complete an order, please allow up to 3 additional business days for material procurement.

GETTING YOUR ORDER TO YOU

Shipping

Where CEEC Ships

CEEC currently ships within the 50 United States, including Alaska and Hawaii.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on the order and delivery destination.

Tracking

Tracking information will be provided when it is available for the selected shipping service.

AFTER SHIPMENT

Delivery Issues

Delayed, Missing, or Damaged Shipments

If a package is delayed, missing, marked as delivered but cannot be located, or arrives damaged in transit, please contact CEEC so we can review the shipment.

When an order is determined to have been lost or damaged in transit, CEEC will replace the affected item or order at no additional charge. We may request information or photographs needed to verify the issue or support a carrier claim.

Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for reviewing their shipping address for accuracy before submitting an order.

If an order cannot be delivered because the customer provided an incorrect or incomplete address, CEEC can resend the order after it is returned to us. Additional processing and shipping charges will apply.

If the shipping error was made by CEEC, we will correct and reship the order at no additional charge.

IF SOMETHING ISN'T RIGHT

Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Return Window

Eligible non-custom items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Returned items must be in their original, unused condition.

Return Shipping

CEEC will cover return shipping for approved returns. Please contact us before sending an item back so we can provide return instructions.

Non-Defective Returns

Approved returns of non-defective items are subject to a 10% restocking fee. The restocking fee does not apply when an item is defective, damaged in transit, or incorrect due to an error by CEEC.

PERSONALIZED PIECES

Custom & Made-to-Order Items

Custom and personalized orders are made specifically for the customer and are considered final sale.

If CEEC made an error while creating the item, we will work with you to correct the order at no additional charge.

WE WANT TO MAKE IT RIGHT

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, appears defective, or you receive an incorrect item, please contact CEEC within 30 days of delivery.

We will review the circumstances on a case-by-case basis and may request photographs or other information to help us evaluate the issue. Eligible claims will be corrected or replaced at no additional charge.

QUESTIONS?

We're happy to help. ♡

If you have a question about an order, shipping, or a return, contact CEEC and include your name and any information that helps identify the order.
