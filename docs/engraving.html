Common Materials

Great candidates

  • Wood (pine, oak, birch, maple, bamboo)
  • Acrylic (cast preferred for crisp edges)
  • Leather (veg‑tan engraves best)
  • Painted / powder‑coated items (we remove the top coat to reveal contrast)
  • Anodized aluminum (marking)
  • Slate / stone (marking)

Common items we do not engrave

  • PVC / vinyl (including “pleather” or vinyl‑coated items)
  • Rubber that produces heavy odor/smoke (case‑by‑case only)
  • Clear polycarbonate (often melts/chars; acrylic is the better substitute)
  • Bare, shiny metals like stainless steel, brass, copper (deep engraving requires different equipment)
  • Unknown plastics without a safe ID stamp (we won’t risk harmful fumes)

Not sure what your item is made of? Send a photo and a link to the product listing (if you have it). We’ll confirm compatibility before you buy.

Artwork & File Types

Vector files (SVG, AI, PDF) are ideal. High‑res PNG/JPG (at least 300 DPI) also works. We can help convert.

How it works

1) Send your idea — A quick description and a reference photo or file gets us started.
2) Proof & approval — We send a layout proof. One round of tweaks is included with base pricing.
3) Engrave & finish — Typical lead time: 2–5 business days. Rush available when possible.
4) Pickup or ship — Local pickup in the CSRA or ship to you (shipping extra).
