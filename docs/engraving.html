Common Materials

Great candidates

  • Wood, including pine, oak, birch, maple, and bamboo
  • Acrylic, with cast acrylic preferred for crisp results
  • Leather, especially vegetable-tanned leather
  • Painted or powder-coated items
  • Anodized aluminum
  • Slate and certain stone products
  • Compatible metals using a laser marking compound when needed

Common limitations

  • PVC, vinyl, and unknown plastics are not accepted because they may release hazardous fumes.
  • Clear polycarbonate often melts or chars and is generally unsuitable.
  • Oversized or sharply curved objects may exceed equipment clearance or rotary limits.
  • Mirror-polished, plated, or delicate finishes require individual review.
  • Deep engraving into bare metal requires equipment different from our current laser system.

Unsure about the material? Include photos, dimensions, and a product link when available.

Artwork & Logos

Vector files such as SVG, AI, and vector PDF are preferred. High-resolution PNG and JPG files may also work. Artwork cleanup or conversion may require additional design time.

Artwork permission

By submitting artwork, logos, characters, or other protected material, the customer confirms that they own the rights or have permission to reproduce it.

Trademark symbols

Please specify whether symbols such as ® or ™ should be included. We will not assume that they are part of the requested engraving.
